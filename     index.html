中国史前文化

Chinese Prehistoric Cultures · 中英文对照介绍

Overview / 简介

中国的新石器时代存在许多区域性文化。下面选择裴李岗、良渚、磁山、仰韶和龙山文化， 从年代、地域、农业、手工业和社会发展等方面进行简要介绍。

China had many regional cultures during the Neolithic period. This page introduces five important archaeological cultures through their dates, locations, agriculture, crafts, and social development.

1. Peiligang Culture

裴李岗文化
约公元前7000–5000年
  • Region: Central Plains, especially Henan.
  • Features: Early farming villages, stone tools and pottery.
  • Culture: Evidence of settled life and food processing.
  • 特色： 是研究中原早期新石器文化发展的重要材料。

2. Liangzhu Culture

良渚文化
约公元前3300–2300年
  • Region: Lower Yangtze River, around present-day Zhejiang.
  • Features: Rice agriculture, planned city, water-control system and jade objects.
  • Society: Evidence of social hierarchy and an early regional state.
  • 特色： 体现了新石器时代晚期较成熟的城市与社会组织。

3. Cishan Culture

磁山文化
约公元前5400–5100年（磁山遗址）
  • Region: Southern Hebei and nearby areas of North China.
  • Features: Agriculture, millet storage, stone farming tools and pottery.
  • Life: People also raised animals and practiced fishing and gathering.
  • 特色： 遗址中发现大量储存粮食的遗迹和农业生产工具。

4. Yangshao Culture

仰韶文化
约公元前5000–3000年
  • Region: Middle Yellow River, including Henan, Shaanxi and Shanxi.
  • Features: Farming villages and distinctive painted pottery.
  • Sites: Banpo is one of the best-known Yangshao sites.
  • 特色： 彩陶是其最具代表性的文化遗存之一。

5. Longshan Culture

龙山文化
约公元前3000–1900年
  • Region: Middle and lower Yellow River areas.
  • Features: Highly polished black pottery, fortified settlements and increasing social differentiation.
  • Development: It belongs to the Late Neolithic period and shows increasingly complex societies.
  • 特色： 黑陶、城址和社会分化是研究龙山文化的重要线索。

Timeline / 时间线

Peiligang → Cishan → Yangshao → Longshan

良渚文化与黄河流域的文化发展并非简单的“先后替代”关系，而是中国不同区域同时发展的重要例子。 Liangzhu developed in the Yangtze River region alongside other regional traditions.
